Choose Wisely

Why hosting choice matters more than most people think

A cheap plan that slows down at the wrong time costs more than it saves. Visitors leave slow pages, and search engines notice that too. Check these five things before you buy anything.

  • Storage type. NVMe drives move data much faster than older disks, which helps page load times.
  • Server software. LiteSpeed with caching handles traffic spikes better than a basic setup.
  • Security. Malware scanning and a firewall protect you from common attacks on WordPress and other CMS sites.
  • Server location. Data centres in India, the USA and the Netherlands let you place your site near your visitors. See the data centre locations.
  • Support. When something breaks at night, a team that answers quickly is worth more than a small discount.
